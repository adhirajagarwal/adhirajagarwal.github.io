I am currently studying *Introductory Abstract Algebra* and *ordinary differential equations*. Below are the primary resources I use for study.
- Jacobson, Nathan. Basic Algebra I. 2nd edition, W.H. Freeman and Company, 1985.
- Rotman, Joseph. The Theory of Groups: An Introduction. 2nd edition, Allyn and Bacon, Inc., 1973
- [Expository papers](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/) by Keith Conrad.
- Arnol'd, Vladimir. Ordinary Differential Equations. 3rd edition, translation by Roger Cooke, Springer-Verlag, 1992.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/)
